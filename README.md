# Liquidation Command Center

**Strategic asset disposition for discerning sellers**

## Overview

A versatile pricing and inventory tool for anyone selling multiple items — whether clearing out a garage, managing an eBay store, liquidating an estate, downsizing before a move, or offloading business equipment.

## The Problem It Solves

Selling multiple items means constantly weighing trade-offs: hold out for top dollar, or price to move? Without a system, sellers either undervalue quality items, overprice slow-movers, or lose track of what they're actually netting. Spreadsheets work but lack purpose-built features; mental math doesn't scale.

## How It Works

The application models **three pricing tiers** for every item simultaneously:

| Scenario | Strategy | When to Use |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Maximum** | Aspirational pricing | You have time and the item has demand |
| **Balanced** | Fair market value | Reasonable offers, steady movement |
| **Quick Sale** | Accept and move on | Prioritize speed over margin |

Real-time dashboards show totals across all three scenarios, so you always know your best-case, expected, and floor outcomes.

## Key Features

- ✅ **Three-tier pricing model** with instant scenario totals
- 📦 **Flexible categories** — electronics, furniture, clothing, collectibles, tools, whatever you're selling
- 🔍 **Search and filter** to manage large inventories
- 🎯 **Drag-and-drop sorting** to organize by priority
- ✔️ **Toggle items in/out** as they sell
- 📝 **Notes field** for condition, listing details, or buyer interest
- 💾 **Export/Import** — back up your data or share with a partner
- 🔒 **Works offline** — data persists in your browser

## Use Cases

## Getting Started

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser. No installation, no dependencies, no server required.

The app comes pre-loaded with sample data to demonstrate its capabilities. You can:
- Add your own items
- Modify prices in real-time
- Filter by category
- Export your data as JSON or CSV
- Reset to defaults anytime

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Your information never leaves your device.

**Important:** Export your data regularly to avoid loss if you clear browser data.

## Target User

Anyone selling 10+ items who wants clarity on what they're working with, what it's worth, and what they'll walk away with under different scenarios.
